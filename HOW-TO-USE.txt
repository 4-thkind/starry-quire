SPIRAL NOTEBOOK — install, use, uninstall
=========================================

INSTALL
  1. Unzip SpiralNotebook.zip and double-click SpiralNotebook-Setup.exe.
  2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC" (the app isn't code-signed),
     click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
  3. Click through the installer. It adds a Desktop and Start-menu shortcut.
     No admin rights needed; it installs just for your user.

HOW IT WORKS
  Cover -> Index (both pages) -> your note pages.
  Open the book  : click the cover (or swipe / press ->)
  Turn a page    : grab a page by its corner or margin and drag it over,
                   like real paper (hover a corner to see it lift), or
                   two-finger swipe on the touchpad, or -> / <- arrow keys,
                   or Page Down / Page Up while typing
  Close the book : turn back past the Index, or press Esc
  Jump to index  : the ☰ button (top-right) or Home
  Open a note    : click its line in the Index
  Click the lines and type. Pages never run out: fresh blank pages keep
  appearing as you go. Everything saves by itself. F11 = full screen.

SHORTCUTS  (Ctrl+/ or the ? button shows these inside the app)
  Ctrl+Shift+H   Heading on the current line: big on a page's first line,
                 smaller anywhere else. Press again to undo. Enter = normal text.
  Ctrl+B / I / U Bold / italic / underline
  Ctrl+Shift+Y   Highlight             Ctrl+Shift+X  Strikethrough
  Ctrl+Shift+8   Bullets  (or type "- " at the start of a line)
  Ctrl+Shift+7   Numbered list  (or type "1. ")
  Tab/Shift+Tab  Indent / outdent a point;  Enter twice leaves a list
  -> <- => <->   Typed, they become  → ← ⇒ ↔
  Ctrl+\         Clear formatting      Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y  Undo / redo

WHERE YOUR NOTES ARE SAVED
  %APPDATA%\spiral-notebook\notes.json
  (paste  %APPDATA%\spiral-notebook  into File Explorer's address bar)
  Copy that file to back up your notes or move them to another PC.

UNINSTALL
  Settings -> Apps -> Installed apps -> Spiral Notebook -> Uninstall
  (or Start menu -> right-click Spiral Notebook -> Uninstall).
  This removes the app, its shortcuts AND ALL YOUR NOTES.
  Back up notes.json first if you want to keep them.
  Installing a newer version over the old one does NOT delete your notes.
